=== Viewer ===

New movie player based on modern APIs:

<WRAP indent>{{:release_history:viewer_video.png?nolink|}}

If you don't see the new player for a video, grab the appropriate codec from Microsoft:

  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9MVZQVXJBQ9V?ocid=pdpshare|AV1 Video Extension]]
  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9N4WGH0Z6VHQ?ocid=pdpshare|HEVC Video Extensions from the Device Manufacturer]]
  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9NMZLZ57R3T7?ocid=pdpshare|HEVC Video Extensions]] (only use if above not available)
  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9N95Q1ZZPMH4?ocid=pdpshare|MPEG-2 Video Extension]]
  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9N4D0MSMP0PT?ocid=pdpshare|VP9 Video Extensions]]
  * [[https://www.microsoft.com/store/productid/9N5TDP8VCMHS?ocid=pdpshare|Web Media Extensions]]
  * Alternatively, DirectShow codecs like [[https://github.com/Nevcairiel/LAVFilters/releases|LAV Filters]] are also supported
</WRAP>\\
