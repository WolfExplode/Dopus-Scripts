~~Title: OnClipboardChange ~~
<pageheader/>

The **OnClipboardChange** event can be implemented by a [[:scripting:script_add-ins|script add-in]] to receive notification whenever the contents of the system clipboard change.


<commandtable columns="2" id="cmdtable_1">
$$ (#)**Method Name:**
$$ OnClipboardChange
$$ (#)**Argument Type:**
$$ **[[..:scripting_objects:clipboardchangedata|ClipboardChangeData]]** 
$$ (#)**Return Type:**
$$ //none//
$$ (#)**Description:**
$$ Your event method will be called whenever the clipboard contents change.
</commandtable>
